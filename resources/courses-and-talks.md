# courses and talks

> video lectures, MOOCs, and conference talks on QML. the QGSS 2021 course is the main one this repo is based on but these r all worth checking out.

---

### Qiskit Global Summer School 2021 --- Quantum Machine Learning

[youtube playlist](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOFEBzvs-VvqJwybFxkTiDzhf5E11p8BI)

this is THE course. 20 lectures + 5 labs covering everything from vector spaces to quantum GANs. taught by researchers from IBM Quantum including amira abbas, christa zoufal, and others. this entire repo is my notes for this course. if u only watch one QML course, watch this one.

---

### IBM Quantum Learning --- QML course

[quantum.cloud.ibm.com](https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/)

ibm's official learning platform has a structured QML course with interactive exercises. good for hands-on practice with qiskit. covers similar ground to QGSS but in a self-paced format with coding challenges.

---

### openHPI --- "Quantum Machine Learning with IBM Quantum"

free MOOC on the openHPI platform. covers the basics of QML with IBM Quantum tools. more beginner-friendly than QGSS and has graded assignments. good if u want smtg structured with deadlines to keep u on track.

---

### PennyLane QML demos and codebook

[pennylane.ai/qml](https://pennylane.ai/qml/) and [pennylane.ai/codebook](https://pennylane.ai/codebook/)

not a traditional course but arguably better for learning by doing. the codebook is a structured set of coding challenges that build up ur understanding step by step. the demos r standalone tutorials on specific QML topics. all runnable in browser. i go back to these constantly.

---

### edX --- University of Toronto QML course

peter wittek's QML course on edX. covers the theoretical foundations of QML from a computer science perspective. more formal and mathematical than QGSS. sadly peter passed away in 2019 but his course material lives on and is still one of the best academic treatments of the subject.

---

### QGSS 2024 --- Meltem Tolunay QML lecture

the 2024 edition of QGSS had updated QML content. meltem tolunay's lecture covers recent developments that happened after the 2021 course --- new results on trainability, better ansatze, updated understanding of quantum advantage claims. good supplement after u finish the 2021 material.

---

### Qiskit YouTube channel

[youtube.com/@qiskit](https://youtube.com/@qiskit)

tons of QML content beyond just QGSS. coding tutorials, research talks, community presentations. the "Coding with Qiskit" series is especially useful for hands-on stuff.

---

### Maria Schuld's QML talks on YouTube

maria's conference talks r some of the best QML content on the internet. she has this ability to explain deep theoretical ideas in a way that actually makes sense. search for her talks at QIP, QTML, and various university seminars. her "is quantum advantage the right goal" talk is a must-watch.

---

### QTML conference recordings

the Quantum Techniques in Machine Learning conference is the main venue for QML research talks. past recordings r available on youtube. cutting edge stuff --- not beginner friendly but invaluable once u have the basics down. check out [qtml2025.cqt.sg](https://qtml2025.cqt.sg/) for the latest edition.

---

### Coding with Qiskit S2E6 --- QML episode

a single focused episode on QML from the Coding with Qiskit series. good 20-minute intro that shows u how to actually implement a quantum classifier in qiskit. not deep but gets u coding fast. watch this if u want to see the code before the math.
