# community

> where to find QML people online. forums, slacks, discords, and people to follow. the QML community is small enough that u can actually talk to the researchers doing the work which is pretty cool.

---

## forums and discussion

### Qiskit Slack workspace

the main place for qiskit discussions. has dedicated channels for QML, variational algorithms, and research. ibm quantum researchers hang out here and actually answer questions. join the #machine-learning channel.

### r/QuantumComputing

[reddit.com/r/QuantumComputing](https://reddit.com/r/QuantumComputing)

general quantum computing subreddit. QML posts come up regularly. quality varies --- some great discussions, some pure hype. good for staying in the loop on news and new papers.

### r/QuantumMachineLearning

[reddit.com/r/QuantumMachineLearning](https://reddit.com/r/QuantumMachineLearning)

dedicated QML subreddit. smaller community but more focused. people share papers, ask technical questions, and discuss implementations. worth subscribing to.

### Quantum Computing Stack Exchange

[quantumcomputing.stackexchange.com](https://quantumcomputing.stackexchange.com/)

the best place to ask technical QML questions and actually get rigorous answers. the community there is knowledgeable and the Q&A format means answers get vetted. ive found answers to some of my weirdest questions here.

### PennyLane Discussion Forum

[discuss.pennylane.ai](https://discuss.pennylane.ai/)

xanadu's forum for PennyLane users. great for implementation questions --- if u r trying to build smtg with PennyLane and getting stuck, post here. the devs r active and helpful.

## open source

### Qiskit Machine Learning

[github.com/qiskit-community/qiskit-machine-learning](https://github.com/qiskit-community/qiskit-machine-learning)

the official qiskit library for QML. has implementations of quantum kernels, variational classifiers, QGANs, and more. good codebase to read if u want to see how the papers translate to actual code. also a good place to contribute.

---

## conferences

### QTML --- Quantum Techniques in Machine Learning

[qtml2025.cqt.sg](https://qtml2025.cqt.sg/)

THE conference for QML research. brings together quantum computing and ML researchers. past editions have recordings on youtube. if u r serious abt QML research, submit a poster or just attend to see wts cutting edge.

## people to follow on twitter/X

these r the researchers and orgs doing the actual work in QML. follow them for paper drops, hot takes, and conference updates.

- **Maria Schuld** (@MariaSchuldPhys) --- literally wrote the book on QML. her takes on quantum advantage and kernel methods r essential reading
- **Amira Abbas** (@AmiraAbbas12) --- power of QNNs, effective dimension, great at connecting theory to practice
- **Christa Zoufal** (@ChristaZoufal) --- QGANs, quantum generative models, applied QML
- **Kristan Temme** (@KristanTemme) --- quantum kernel advantage, error mitigation, rigorous proofs
- **Jarrod McClean** (@JarrodMcClean) --- barren plateaus, variational algorithms, now at Google Quantum AI
- **PennyLane** (@PennyLaneAI) --- xanadu's QML framework, shares demos and research updates
- **Qiskit** (@qaborosa) --- ibm's quantum SDK, shares tutorials and community highlights
