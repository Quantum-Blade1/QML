# blogs and posts

> the best engineering blogs and researcher posts on QML. not random medium spam --- actual good stuff from people who know wt theyre talking abt.

---

### 1. Maria Schuld --- "Is quantum advantage the right goal for QML?"

maria schuld is honestly the most clear-headed thinker in QML rn. her talks and blog posts cut through the hype and ask the real questions --- like whether chasing "quantum advantage" is even the right framing for the field. she literally wrote the textbook on this. if u follow one person in QML, make it her.

---

### 2. Amira Abbas --- talks and work on power of QNNs

amira's work on effective dimension and the power of quantum neural networks is some of the most important recent QML research. her talks at IBM Quantum events break down complex expressibility arguments into smtg u can actually follow. also shes great at connecting theory to practice.

---

### 3. PennyLane QML demos page

[pennylane.ai/qml](https://pennylane.ai/qml/)

this is lowkey one of the best learning resources for QML. xanadu maintains a huge collection of interactive demos covering everything from basic variational circuits to cutting edge research implementations. each demo has code u can run, math explanations, and references. ive spent hours here tbh.

---

### 4. Qiskit Medium blog --- QML related posts

the qiskit team puts out solid technical posts on medium. their QML posts cover things like implementing quantum kernels, building variational classifiers, and understanding noise effects on QML. practical and well-written. good complement to the more theoretical papers.

---

### 5. IBM Research blog --- QML and quantum kernel posts

the IBM research blog goes deeper than the qiskit medium posts. they cover their own papers and explain the intuition behind results like quantum kernel advantage and trainability issues. good for understanding the "so wt does this actually mean" behind the arxiv papers.

---

### 6. Christa Zoufal --- QGAN work and blog posts

christa's work on quantum GANs is some of the coolest applied QML out there. her posts and talks explain how u can use quantum circuits for generative modeling --- loading probability distributions, generating financial data, etc. very practical angle on QML.

---

### 7. Kristan Temme --- quantum kernel advantage papers

temme is behind some of the most rigorous quantum advantage proofs in QML (the Liu-Arunachalam-Temme paper especially). his blog posts and talks give u the intuition behind these complexity-theoretic results without drowning u in notation. important if u care abt whether QML advantages r real.

---

### 8. The Quantum Insider --- QML explainers

[thequantuminsider.com](https://thequantuminsider.com/)

decent tech journalism on quantum computing. their QML explainers r accessible and cover industry developments. not as technical as papers or researcher blogs but good for staying updated on wts happening in the field. take the hype with a grain of salt tho.

---

### 9. Quantum Zeitgeist --- PennyLane and QML articles

[quantumzeitgeist.com](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/)

covers QML news and tutorials, especially around the PennyLane ecosystem. good for keeping up with new demos, library updates, and conference announcements. more news-oriented than technical but useful for the big picture.

---

### 10. Jay Pandit --- "Quantum Machine Learning: A Complete Guide" (Medium)

a solid long-form guide that walks through QML concepts from scratch. good for getting the intuition before diving into papers. not super deep mathematically but covers the landscape well and connects the dots between diff approaches. useful as a first read.
